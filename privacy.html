Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026 · Compliant with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

SIA Coding Capital is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we process when you visit www.coding-capital.com, why, on what legal basis, and the rights you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we process

This is an informational website. We keep data collection to a minimum.

a. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message solely to respond to your request. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and, where relevant, pre-contractual steps (Art. 6(1)(b)).

b. Server and access logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Our host may automatically record technical data such as your IP address, browser type, referring page and access times, in order to deliver the site and ensure its security. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

c. Web fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google servers, which may process your IP address to deliver the font files. Legal basis: legitimate interest in presenting the site consistently (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Privacy note: to avoid transmitting visitors' IP addresses to Google, these fonts can be self-hosted directly on the website. Ask us if you would like this change applied.

We do not use advertising, tracking or profiling technologies, and we do not sell your data.

3. Recipients of your data

Your data may be processed by our technical service providers acting as processors, in particular our hosting provider GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) and Google (font delivery). We do not share your data with any other third party except where required by law.

4. International transfers

Some providers (GitHub, Google) are established in the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, such transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. Data retention

Email correspondence is kept for as long as necessary to handle your request and, where applicable, to meet our legal obligations. Technical logs are retained by our host for a limited period.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to: access your data; request rectification or erasure; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@coding-capital.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Latvia, this is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija) — www.dvi.gov.lv.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
