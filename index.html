The group

A technology holding company with an operator's mindset.

Coding Capital does more than invest. We work alongside the companies in which we hold interests, turning specialised expertise into robust, scalable and commercially sustainable software products.

Our portfolio spans several European jurisdictions and combines autonomous operating companies with a shared layer of strategic, technological and entrepreneurial expertise.

01

Equity with involvement

We take an active role in the ventures we support — contributing decisions, systems and execution, not merely capital.

02

Specialised vertical software

We focus on markets where complex information, fragmented workflows and professional expertise can be transformed through software.

03

Capital-efficient growth

We favour durable economics, disciplined execution and businesses that grow without excessive organisational complexity.

R

Rémi de Truchis de Varennes

CO-FOUNDER · STRATEGY & CAPITAL

Rémi is a French engineer, software entrepreneur and capital allocator. He has founded and co-founded a portfolio of profitable digital businesses across software, financial technology, real-estate intelligence and professional decision-support systems.

His experience spans market positioning, product strategy, digital distribution, partnerships, corporate finance and long-term value creation. He is AMF-certified, a published author with Hachette and a regular contributor to French business media.

E

Estelle Jacot

CO-FOUNDER · TECHNOLOGY & PRODUCT

Estelle is a French engineer, software entrepreneur and technology executive specialising in the creation and scaling of data-intensive digital products. A graduate of SUPMICROTECH-ENSMM, she combines rigorous engineering training with hands-on software and product-architecture expertise.

After working as an independent developer and within the MedTech environment, she co-founded Value Investing Screener and Coding Capital. She leads product architecture, engineering strategy, data infrastructure, automation and applied AI across the group's ventures.
